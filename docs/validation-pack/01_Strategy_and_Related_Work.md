# The contribution to earn

The most useful complement to the build is to prevent a credible implementation from becoming an undifferentiated demonstration. My strategic recommendation is to focus WorkWorld on operational recovery and AI assistance, and to earn claims through independent evidence. This is a judgment, not a prediction of recruitment, investment or sales.

## One precise proposition

**WorkWorld tests whether people using AI actually complete and recover business workflows, rather than merely producing convincing deliverables.**

The first commercial problem to test: an instructor or operations-training lead cannot reliably tell whether a participant has completed the underlying workflow correctly, particularly after a change, without spending substantial time inspecting the work.

The research question: does assistance change the probability of a correct terminal state when requirements change, and what oversight, correction and assessor work does it require?

Keep the three existing families and six episodes. Use the engine's scheduled changes and existing modes. Do not rebuild the architecture around this question.

## What can plausibly distinguish this project

The contribution to test is the combination of a narrowly specified operations role, inspectable typed state, actual human and assisted participation, recovery after disclosed policy/requirement changes, and measured assessment/oversight effort. Any one of those features can exist elsewhere. The combination must be examined against current implementations and practitioner needs before a novelty claim.

The important result may be negative. If assistance makes summaries more persuasive but does not improve correct completion, a carefully reproduced account of why is more useful than a selectively edited success demo. If the effect is neutral, report neutral. If the tasks are too artificial to distinguish competence, fix the task specification before comparing models.

## One memorable demonstration

1. Show the authorized work goal and starting business state.
2. Show a requirement change that invalidates the current plan.
3. Show the actual actions taken and the records that did or did not change.
4. Show criterion-level assessment, including a polished but incorrect submission if observed.
5. Show the competent recovery and the evidence supporting it.

Use a real run. A scripted illustration can explain grading but must be labelled a scripted control, not model performance. Do not splice independent runs into one apparent recovery.

## Recruiting and funding need different evidence

For a research or applied-agent reviewer, prioritize a reproducible environment, grader negative controls, a precise methods note and a useful failure analysis. A second machine reproducing the results is stronger than another feature page. OpenAI's GDPval introduction explicitly invites industry-expert participation [S01]; that is a contribution route to evaluate, not a recruitment promise or an endorsement.

For a buyer or investor, prioritize a real budget owner, measured assessor/authoring effort, a bounded paid pilot and a plausible second deployment that requires substantially less custom work. A novel benchmark without distribution or favorable delivery economics does not establish a company.

Scott's education and integration experience may help obtain relevant feedback. Familiar institutions and professional relationships are not customers, approved channels or permission to use employer materials. Start with synthetic examples and separately authorized conversations.

## Decisions that protect time

- If independent practitioners cannot agree on the correct outcomes, invest in policy clarity and rubric calibration before a model comparison.
- If buyers want simulations but see no value over free alternatives, do not assume more scenarios will create willingness to pay. Test assessment workload and auditability explicitly.
- If every pilot requires extensive bespoke authoring, position it as a service experiment until repeatability exists; do not use SaaS margins by assumption.
- If another benchmark already measures the same question well, consider a targeted contribution or compatible task pack instead of a broad replacement.
- If the six episodes are trivial for both people and agents, add causal or policy difficulty inside the same role, not more interface features.

No immediate app rebrand, pricing page or portfolio rewrite is required. The next proof should determine the story.
