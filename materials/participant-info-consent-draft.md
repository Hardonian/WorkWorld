# Participant Information and Consent (DRAFT — for review; not approved)

Status: **draft for governance review**. No ethics/IRB approval exists or is
claimed. No participant data has been collected. Do not recruit or enroll anyone
using this draft.

## Study title

Operational work with and without AI assistance: a feasibility study in a
simulated small-business environment (WorkWorld).

## Purpose

We are investigating when AI assistance improves completed operational work and
when it improves only how the work appears. You will complete simulated
operations tasks (orders, deliveries, invoices, tickets) in a persistent
environment. We record your actions, artifacts, and timing.

## What participation involves

- 1–2 hours: 2–3 simulated work episodes in one of three conditions
  (human-only, AI-assisted, or AI-led with your review), plus a short questionnaire.
- Optional follow-up interview (~30 min).
- All company data is synthetic. No real money, customers, or suppliers are involved.

## What we record

Actions taken in the environment, artifacts you produce (plans, messages,
spreadsheets), logical-time and active work-time, help requests, AI suggestions
(if assisted condition) and whether you accepted them, and your questionnaire
responses. Browser/UI telemetry is limited to functional events needed for
reliability analysis.

## Risks and limits

- Minimal risk: simulated work; possible mild frustration; no deception about
  your performance (you receive outcome evidence).
- Your results are **not** an employment assessment, certification, or verified
  skill claim. Outputs are for research on human-AI work patterns.
- AI suggestions may be wrong. You retain responsibility for your work.

## Voluntary, confidential, and removable

Participation is voluntary; you may stop at any time without consequence.
Data is identified by a participant code; direct identifiers are stored
separately. We will not publish identifiable trajectories. You may request
export or deletion of your data under the pilot's actual data policy
(policy to be finalized before any collection).

## Reuse of trajectories (separate consent)

Any reuse of your task trajectories for research publication, model training,
or product improvement beyond this study is **separately consented**; absence of
that consent excludes your data from such reuse. Default: no reuse.
