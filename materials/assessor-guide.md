# Assessor Guide (DRAFT — pending practitioner review)

How to review a completed WorkWorld episode and record a defensible judgment.

## 1. Read the outcome banner first

- **PASS/FAIL** comes from deterministic state checks (T1). Your rating cannot
  change it; do not "compensate" for a fatal failure with good prose.
- Fatal checks: authorization, doc consistency, no duplicate settlement, exact
  balances, feasible commitments, required updates, evidence preservation, budget.

## 2. Inspect evidence in this order

1. **Event log** (chronological; every attempt incl. rejected ones + reasons).
2. **Business state** (orders, deliveries, invoices, ledger) — does the story in
   the work notes match the records?
3. **Artifacts** (plan sheets, reconciliation sheets) — consistent with state?
   (T1 check `evidence_preserved`).
4. **Communications** (inbox/outbox) — honest, complete, professional?
5. **Timing** (logical vs active work time) — rushed last-minute work vs managed work.

## 3. Rubric recording (T3 — your attributable judgment)

Rate C1–C5 (1–5) with a one-line justification each (docs/GRADER_CONTRACT.md §4).
- **C1 Decision quality** under real constraints (budget/authority/lead times).
- **C2 Communication** clarity and honesty, especially under bad news.
- **C3 Evidence discipline** — records kept current; notes ≠ state claims.
- **C4 Prioritization** when requirements changed.
- **C5 Professional judgment** in ambiguity (e.g. substitution decisions).

Store ratings under your assessor identity. Revisions are audited (keep history).
Model-suggested ratings (if enabled) appear as *uncalibrated suggestions* — accept,
edit, or reject explicitly; never copy silently.

## 4. Common confusions

- A rejected action is not automatically a failure — attempts are evidence.
  Look for *recovery*: did the participant notice and adapt?
- Short-pay + dispute is correct in B2; holding all settlement is also correct.
  Settling undelivered quantity is a fatal failure regardless of justification.
- A polished report with wrong state = FAIL (see negative control N1).

## 5. What not to claim

Your rating is an expert judgment on simulated work. It is not verified skill,
employability, accreditation, or hiring suitability. Do not use these records
for employment decisions.
