## What we are asking a reviewer to do

Time: ~60–90 minutes. Review the three scenario families and six episodes of a
small-business operations apprenticeship (purchase/delivery, invoice/reconciliation,
customer/project recovery) and answer:

1. **Realism** — Would an operations coordinator at a ~20-person supplier face
   these decisions? What is wrong or missing? (1–5 + comments per family)
2. **Policy realism** — Are the authority thresholds (CAD 400 single-order,
   CAD 2500 episode budget), matching rules (three-way match, short-pay,
   duplicate handling) plausible for a small business? What would you change?
3. **Consequential events** — Are the mid-task changes (delay, substitution,
   requirement change, shortfall) representative of what actually disrupts work?
4. **Outcome checks** — For each episode's fatal checks: would a competent
   coordinator agree these mark real failure? Any false-fail/false-pass risks?
5. **Rubric** — Are C1–C5 the right subjective criteria for this role? What is
   missing? Would you sign an assessment based on this rubric?
6. **Difficulty** — Roughly how long would each episode take a new coordinator?
   Which is hardest, and is the difficulty ordering sensible?
